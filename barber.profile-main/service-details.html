Professional Barber Services

At KIKIBON BLADE AND FADES, we provide premium barber services with precision cuts, expert fades, and personalized styling. Each service is crafted to bring out your best look with attention to detail and professional expertise.

Professional Hair Cut - P150-250

Our signature hair cut service features precision cutting techniques combined with modern styling trends. Whether you're looking for a classic look, a contemporary fade, or a trendy style, our expert barbers will craft the perfect cut tailored to your hair type and facial features.

  • Precision scissor work and blade expertise
  • Personalized consultation and styling advice
  • Modern and classic style options available
  • Quality grooming products and finishing touches

We take pride in every haircut, ensuring you leave feeling confident and looking sharp. Our barbers stay updated with the latest trends while respecting your personal style preferences. Each cut is an opportunity to showcase our commitment to excellence.

Expert Fade Styles - P150-200

From low fades to high fades and everything in between, our barbers specialize in creating clean, precise fade cuts. We offer various fade styles including taper fades, skin fades, and textured fades that complement any hairstyle and personal preference.

  • Low, mid, and high fade options
  • Skin fades for the sharpest look
  • Textured and blended fade variations
  • Clean edge work and finishing details

Our fade specialists use professional clippers and techniques to create smooth transitions and sharp lines. We pay careful attention to symmetry and proportions to ensure your fade looks perfect from every angle. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Beard Trim & Grooming - P100

Complete your look with our professional beard trimming and grooming service. We shape, trim, and groom your beard to perfection, enhancing your facial features and maintaining a well-groomed appearance.

  • Expert beard shaping and styling
  • Clean line work around the face and neck
  • Beard health and maintenance advice
  • Precision trimming for all beard styles

A well-groomed beard is a statement of style and confidence. Our barbers understand different beard types and growth patterns, providing customized grooming solutions that work for you. Let us help you achieve the beard look you've always wanted.

Home Service Haircut - P500

Can't make it to our barbershop? No problem! We bring our professional barber services directly to your home. Enjoy a premium haircut experience in the comfort and convenience of your own space with our home service option.

  • Professional barber visits your home
  • Same quality service as our barbershop
  • Flexible scheduling at your convenience
  • All professional tools and equipment provided

Perfect for busy professionals, families, or anyone who prefers the convenience of home service. We maintain the same high standards of cleanliness and professionalism as our main location. Book your home service appointment today and experience premium barber care at your doorstep.
